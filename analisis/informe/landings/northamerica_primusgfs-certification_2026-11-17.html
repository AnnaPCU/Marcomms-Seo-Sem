PrimusGFS v3.2 and v4.0: Which Version Applies to Your Audit?

Párrafo
Cargar

PrimusGFS audits are conducted against the version in force on your audit date. Version 4.0 keeps the modular structure and audit duration of v3.2 and adds requirements on food safety culture, crisis management, agricultural water, harvest equipment sanitation and FSMA 204 traceability. The date when v4.0 becomes mandatory depends on GFSI benchmarking; CUC USA confirms the applicable version when scheduling your audit.
