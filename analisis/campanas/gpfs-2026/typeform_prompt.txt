Create a Typeform called "Control Union | Fresh Produce Certification Check" for attendees of The Global Produce & Floral Show (Orlando, Oct 14-16, 2026). In 2 minutes, produce and flower businesses see which certification programs match their operation.

BRAND AND TONE
- Control Union: accredited certification body, 100+ years, 80+ countries, 200+ programs. Always "Control Union", never a country office.
- Formal, technical, clear. No emojis or exclamation marks.
- Use certify, audit, inspect, verify. Never: advise, consult, prepare, implement, gap analysis, readiness score, guaranteed.
- Buttons #3eb2ed, text #1b1e42, accents #799495, white background, Ubuntu font.
- English, one question per screen, progress bar on.

WELCOME
Title: "Which certifications does your produce business need?"
Text: "Answer 10 quick questions and see the programs that match your operation."
Button: "Start the check"

QUESTIONS
1. Which best describes your operation? (single) Grower or farm / Packer or packinghouse / Cooler or distribution / Exporter or importer / Processor / Flower grower / Other
2. What do you grow or handle? (multiple) Fruit / Vegetables / Flowers and plants / Organic products / Other
3. Where do you sell? (multiple) North America / Europe / UK / Asia / Latin America / Domestic only
4. What are your buyers asking for? (multiple) Food safety audit (GFSI) / Good agricultural practices / Organic / Social or ethical audit / Sustainable flowers / Packaging / Fair trade / Not sure yet
5. Which certifications do you hold today? (multiple) PrimusGFS / GLOBALG.A.P. / USDA Organic (NOP) / SMETA / GRASP / BRCGS / Florverde / Fair Trade USA / IFS / None yet
6. How many audits do you have each year? (single) None / 1-2 / 3-4 / 5 or more
7. Main priority right now? (single) A new buyer requirement / Fewer audits / A new market / Renewing or changing certification body
8. When do you need a certificate? (single) Within 3 months / 3-6 months / 6-12 months / Just exploring
9. Will you be at the show in Orlando? (single) Thursday Oct 15 / Friday Oct 16 / Both days / Not this year
10. Contact: first name, last name, company, job title, business email (required), country, phone (optional).
11. Required checkbox: "I agree that Control Union may contact me about this request." Link: https://northamerica.controlunion.com/privacy-policy-usa/

LOGIC (first match wins)
1) Q6 is 3-4 or 5 or more -> Several audits
2) Q4 includes Organic -> Organic
3) Q4 includes Social or Fair trade -> Social
4) Q4 includes Sustainable flowers, or Q1 is Flower grower -> Flowers
5) Q4 includes Food safety, Good agricultural practices or Packaging -> Food safety
6) Otherwise -> General
